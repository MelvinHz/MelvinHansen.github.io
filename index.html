User Preference Topic Classification

Year
2026
Role
Group project, Researcher & Paper Author

Project description

We built an NLP program that recommends a user's interests by detecting the topic of the text they write every day. We trained and compared four models on 45,000 texts across 9 topics, then deployed the best one, TF-IDF with a linear SVM (82.22% accuracy), as a Streamlit app.

Impact & learning

This research showed us how much text representation matters: adding TF-IDF raised accuracy from 42.78% with Naive Bayes to 82.22% with SVM. We also learned why Random Forest struggles with sparse, high-dimensional text, how preprocessing choices affect meaning (such as keeping "Apple" and "apple" apart), and how to choose a model using accuracy and f1-score.

  • NLP
  • TF-IDF
  • SVM
  • Streamlit
  • Research paper
